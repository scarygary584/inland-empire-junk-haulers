Flag these items

  • Paint, chemicals, fuel, or solvents
  • Batteries, electronics, and appliances
  • Tires, concrete, dirt, or construction debris

Why disposal questions matter

Providers may need to confirm whether an item can be transported, where it can be disposed of, and whether extra labor or special handling is required. Photos and clear item descriptions help avoid confusion before pickup is scheduled.

If an item may be hazardous, leaking, sharp, wet, or unusually heavy, mention it before a provider arrives. This site does not provide disposal, cleanup, sanitation, pest control, or code enforcement services.

Provider-handled decisions

Item acceptance, pricing, scheduling, removal, disposal, cleanup, and service terms are confirmed by the provider. Availability may depend on local rules, item type, access, and provider schedule.
