How much does junk removal cost in Hemet?

Pricing can depend on load size, item type, weight, access, labor, travel, and disposal requirements. Photos and a clear item list can help a provider review the job before confirming a price. See the Hemet junk removal pricing guide for more detail.

Can I request same-day junk removal in Hemet?

Yes. Same-day requests can be submitted, especially for clear-access furniture, appliances, curbside items, and smaller loads. Availability must be confirmed for the requested day.

What items can junk haulers pick up?

Common requests include couches, mattresses, dressers, tables, chairs, refrigerators, washers, dryers, garage clutter, boxes, patio furniture, yard items, and mixed household junk. Review the item guide for common examples.

Can mattresses and furniture be removed from inside a home?

Inside pickup can be requested. Share details about stairs, narrow hallways, gates, elevators, parking, and the room where the items are located.

Can old appliances be removed?

Requests may include refrigerators, washers, dryers, ovens, dishwashers, freezers, and water heaters. Mention whether appliances are disconnected and whether stairs or tight access are involved.

Do you handle garage cleanouts and move-out junk?

Garage, rental, estate, and move-out cleanout requests can include boxes, furniture, appliances, shelving, tools, patio items, and mixed clutter. Photos are useful for showing the approximate load size.

Paint, chemicals, fuel, propane, batteries, medical waste, asbestos, contaminated materials, and some other restricted items may require special disposal or may not be accepted. Confirm unusual items before pickup.

What information should I provide?
