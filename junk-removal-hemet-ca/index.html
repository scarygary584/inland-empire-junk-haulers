Common Hemet junk removal requests

Customers often ask about couches, mattresses, refrigerators, washers, dryers, garage clutter, moving boxes, office furniture, patio items, scrap wood, and small outdoor piles. Some items may need provider review before pickup can be confirmed, especially heavy appliances, electronics, tires, paint, chemicals, construction debris, or anything that may require special disposal.

Availability may depend on location, load size, item type, access, disposal rules, weather, and provider schedule. Pricing, scheduling, removal, disposal, cleanup, and service terms are handled by the provider.
