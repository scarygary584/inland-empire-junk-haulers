Furniture, Appliance, and Mattress Removal in San Jacinto

Have a couch, old refrigerator, or mattress you need removed in San Jacinto? Start your pickup request with a list of what needs to go and where the items are located.

Furniture removal

Include sofas, sectionals, recliners, dressers, dining tables, chairs, and desks in your request. Mention whether large pieces are already disassembled or must fit through narrow doors, hallways, or stairways.

Appliance removal

For refrigerators, freezers, washers, dryers, dishwashers, and stoves, say whether the appliance is disconnected and ready for pickup. Flag anything still connected to water, gas, or electricity so any preparation requirements can be confirmed. Refrigerant-containing appliances may require special handling.

Mattress removal

Tell us the mattress size and whether you also have a box spring, bed frame, or adjustable base. Note whether everything is upstairs, inside a bedroom, in a garage, or outside.

Other bulky items

Your request can also include shelving, exercise equipment, patio furniture, or boxed household clutter. Describe unusually heavy items separately. Identify paint, chemicals, batteries, and other materials requiring special disposal so acceptance can be checked before pickup.

Photos can help show the amount of material and any access obstacles. Keep a few clear pictures ready when discussing your request. Accepted items, availability, and pricing must be confirmed before you agree to service.
