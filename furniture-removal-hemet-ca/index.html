Questions about removal services

Can I include mattresses with the furniture?

Yes, you can request both together. Give the size and quantity, include box springs separately, and disclose wetness or pest exposure. Mattress acceptance and any handling requirements must be confirmed before the appointment.

Is furniture pickup the same as a moving service?

No. Hauling junk away for disposal or possible reuse differs from moving possessions to a new home. If you want a usable piece delivered to another address, explain that explicitly and ask whether the provider offers those services.

Does junk removal include cleaning the room?

Do not assume detailed cleaning is included. Ask whether the quoted work covers only taking the agreed pieces away or also gathering small debris and sweeping. Carpet cleaning, repairs, and treatment of contamination need separate discussion.

Can items be picked up from a storage unit?

Ask about availability and provide the unit size, floor, gate hours, and distance from parking. Arrange authorized access and separate the items to keep. Do not leave a crew to guess which contents are junk.

What if a piece is too large for the doorway?

Send measurements and explain the route before scheduling. The provider can review whether safe disassembly or another approach is possible. Do not assume doors, windows, or fixtures will be removed as part of the job.
